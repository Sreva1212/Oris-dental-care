Treatments

Everything from routine cleaning to restoring missing teeth, under one roof.

  • Cavity Restorations

    Decayed areas are cleaned and filled to bring back the tooth's shape and strength.

  • Crowns & Bridges

    Caps for damaged teeth and fixed replacements that fill the gap left by missing ones.

  • Root Canal Treatment

    Removes infection from inside the tooth to relieve pain and save the natural tooth.

  • Dental Implants

    Long-lasting replacements for missing teeth, anchored in the jawbone.

  • Scaling & Polishing

    Removes plaque and tartar, leaving teeth smooth, clean and gums healthier.

  • Tooth Extractions

    Careful removal of teeth that are badly damaged, infected or causing problems.

  • Smile Designing & Correction

    Reshaping and aligning teeth to improve the look of your smile.
